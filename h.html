Chair Yoga
Chair yoga is a modified form of yoga that can be practiced while seated in a chair or using the chair for support during standing poses.
Vinyasa Yoga
Vinyasa yoga, also called "flow" because of the smooth way the poses run together, is one of the most popular contemporary styles of yoga.
Hatha Yoga
Hatha Yoga is a preparatory process of Yoga.
The word “ha” means sun, “ta” means moon.
“Hatha” means the Yoga to bring balance between the sun and the moon.

Chakras Yoga
Chakras are the energy centers of the body. They are located in the astral body, along the spine, starting at its base and running upwards to the crown of the head.
Meditation Yoga
Meditation in yoga involves the practice of calming the mind, bringing awareness to the present moment, and cultivating mindfulness.
Mudras Yoga
Yoga mudras are special hand gestures or positions that help direct the flow of energy in the body. The word “mudra” means “seal” or “gesture” in Sanskrit.

Kundalini Yoga
Kundalini Yoga is often referred to as the “Yoga of Awareness.” Its purpose is to awaken the Kundalini energy, a powerful spiritual energy coiled like a serpent at the base of your spine (in the Muladhara or root chakra).
Jivamukti Yoga
Jivamukti yoga is a path to enlightenment through compassion for all beings. The Jivamukti method is grounded in the original meaning of the sanskrit word asana AS “SEAT, CONNECTION” - Relationship to the Earth.
Ashtanga Yoga
Ashtanga Yoga is a traditional and disciplined style of yoga that involves a series of specific postures, synchronized with breath, in a flowing sequence.
